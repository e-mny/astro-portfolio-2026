A personal portfolio website built to showcase projects with a focus on performance and simplicity.

## Why I made it

![My Bento Website](/projects/portfolio_website_v1_3.png)

<figcaption>My Bento Personal Website</figcaption>

Having a portfolio felt necessary, and I wanted something fast and decent-looking without overengineering it.

## What it does

- Displays projects and write-ups using a static site structure
- Emphasizes performance and minimal load times
- Experiments with different layouts and design inspirations

## Notes

- Spent more time evaluating frameworks than actually building the site
- Decision paralysis (Next.js → Astro → 11ty → SvelteKit → back again) slowed progress significantly
- Early focus on aesthetics over functionality led to unfinished iterations
- Settling on a simple static approach with Astro made development much more practical
