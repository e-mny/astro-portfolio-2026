## What it is

![Banner of Project MONAI](/projects/MONAI_Banner.png)

<figcaption>Adapted from their GitHub page.</figcaption>

[Project MONAI](https://github.com/Project-MONAI) is a collaborative initiative led by NVIDIA and King's College London that brings together academic and industry researchers to advance AI in medical imaging.

It offers a suite of open-source, PyTorch-based tools that support the full AI workflow-from data annotation to model deployment—while emphasizing reproducibility and collaboration.

Key components include:

- **MONAI Core** for training models
- **MONAI Label** for data annotation
- **MONAI Deploy App SDK** for building applications
- **MONAI Model Zoo** for ready-to-use models
