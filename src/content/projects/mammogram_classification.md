## What it is

A machine learning model for early-stage breast cancer detection using mammogram image classification.

## Why I made it

![Photo with my OFYP Professor](/projects/OFYP_ProfYasmeen.jpeg)

<figcaption>Prof Yasmeen George - my FYP supervisor in Monash University</figcaption>

It was my final year project, and I wanted to explore applying AI to real-world medical diagnosis.

## What it does

- Classifies mammogram images to detect potential breast cancer cases
- Applies deep learning techniques to handle medical imaging data
- Uses Grad-CAM for model interpretability and visualization

## Notes

- Medical imaging introduced a steep learning curve, especially in understanding domain-specific nuances
- Dataset imbalance (more negative than positive cases) required techniques like augmentation and class weighting
- Model overfitting was a persistent issue before tuning stabilized performance
- If revisited, I would prioritize more diverse datasets and stronger interpretability methods earlier in development
