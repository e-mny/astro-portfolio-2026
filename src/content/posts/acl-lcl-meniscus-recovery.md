9th August. Singapore's National Day.

Also the day someone decided to knock into me while playing at the finals of an Ultimate Frisbee competition - I then landed wrongly and that took me out for the rest of the season.

Fully torn ACL and LCL, with some damage to my meniscus as well.

Welp.

![MRI of my injured knee](/posts/acl_mri.jpg)

---

## Physio is my sport now

Currently writing this 7 weeks post-op, and slowly getting my range of motion and sanity back.

Been doing physio everyday for close to 2 months. _Just_ another 10 more to go before I hopefully get back to playing sports.

I think the frustrating part is wanting to get back to doing things, but knowing that this is going to take time. Can't rush this recovery because that will lead to further swelling and regressing of things.

For now, getting a little more bend back is a win. Small things, but I'll take them.

---

## I made an app

And yeah... somewhere along the way I made a rehab tracker.

I wanted somewhere to log my measurements and actually see the small wins. It's easy to feel like nothing much is happening when you're doing physio every day, so having something to look back at helps.

It's called [Stride](/projects/acl_rehab_tracker), and it keeps my knee measurements, range of motion, notes and progress photos in one place. There are charts too, because apparently I still need to look at data outside of work.

Runs as a Telegram bot for easy access.

At least something came out of all this time away from the field.

---

Still a long way to go. I miss playing frisbee, and I miss being able to just go and play without thinking so much about my knee.

But for now, more physio, more small wins, and hopefully a little more sanity along the way.
