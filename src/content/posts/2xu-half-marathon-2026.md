![2XU Finisher's Medal 2026](/posts/2XU_1.JPG)

Running 21.1km with 3 hours of sleep ain't it.

Wake up at 1.30am to catch the chartered bus at 2.45am, mad sia.

We arrived at the venue at 3.30am and it was already people mountain people sea.

_Have never seen so many people gather at a place in the middle of the night_

---

## oN YoUR RiGHTT

Skipping ahead to the actual run, it was an alright start.

One thing that bugged me was people walking on the most right lane. Like I think it's fine if you want to walk but can you be considerate to other people and not block the way?

I had to like ninja through the crowd at the start grrr

---

## Nature Called

I was feeling quite fresh but had to empty my bladder really badly, and all the toilets at the hydration points had really long queue.

I remembered there was an area that we always go for a quick drink when we run that area (around the 13km mark). That became my motivation.

But wow, I think I ran faster after denying my bladder hahah.

I got so much energy hearing from the supporters (they were really amazing) and I breezed through the last 5km of the run.

---

![Photo of Marina Bay Sands, taken during my run](/posts/2XU_3.JPG)

I stopped to take a photo of MBS together with the sunrise in the backdrop.

I thought it'll just be another photo, but when I checked my gallery after the run, I was wowed at how amazing it looked.

It kinda looked like I took it from Pinterest or something.

And finally, the finish line. I finished it so strong and well, I was pretty sure I still had some energy left in the tank.

But again, credits to the supporters because yall really gave the energy boost we needed.

![Total steps and km walked that day](/posts/2XU_5.PNG)

After the race, we went back home before continuing on with our day (church, frisbee).

This was my final step count and distance walked for the day - all with less than 5 hours of sleep.

My legs were pleading that I take a break and hence I requested for WFH the next day.

But I thank God for good weather, no runner's knee, blisters or injuries at the end of the day!
